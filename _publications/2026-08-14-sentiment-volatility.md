## Abstract

Tests when media sentiment improves realized volatility forecasts for DJIA stocks, augmenting HAR models with Thomson Reuters/Refinitiv MarketPsych sentiment indices. Augmenting HAR with sentiment yields only a modest *average* improvement, but this average masks strong state dependence: sentiment becomes materially more informative in the upper tail of the volatility distribution and, more importantly, when volatility persistence weakens, measured within a fractional integration framework. Out-of-sample gains concentrate in regimes jointly characterized by high volatility and low persistence, indicating that sentiment supplies forward-looking information precisely when price-based models are least reliable.
