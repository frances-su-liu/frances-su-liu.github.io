I am a PhD Candidate in Finance at the [University of Technology Sydney](https://www.uts.edu.au/about/faculties/business/research/finance-phd/su-frances-liu), supervised by Assoc. Prof. [Vitali Alexeev](https://profiles.uts.edu.au/Vitali.Alexeev) (UTS), Assoc. Prof. [Katja Ignatieva](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/katja-ignatieva) (UNSW) and Prof. [Adam Clements](https://www.qut.edu.au/about/our-people/academic-profiles/a.clements) (QUT).

I work on how machine learning and econometrics can help investors make better use of the data markets produce, from the flow of individual trades to the news that moves prices. The questions I care about are practical ones: which signals are real, how to build portfolios around them, and how to see risk coming.

My current papers, listed under [Publications](/publications/), ask when unconventional data add genuine predictive value, and how to tell that value apart from the effect of a more flexible model.

Before the PhD, I spent six years as a quantitative analyst in China, building systematic equity strategies, portfolio allocation products and derivatives pricing models, along with the research infrastructure behind them. That experience shapes how I judge research: a method has to work on real data and in real portfolios.

I expect to complete my PhD in 2027 and am open to postdoctoral and industry research positions.
