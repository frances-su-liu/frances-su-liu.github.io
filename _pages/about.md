I am a PhD Candidate in Finance at the [University of Technology Sydney](https://www.uts.edu.au/about/faculties/business/research/finance-phd/su-frances-liu) (UTS), supervised by Assoc. Prof. Vitali Alexeev (UTS) and Assoc. Prof. Katja Ignatieva (UNSW).

My research asks when and how different information sets sharpen volatility forecasts, combining high-frequency market microstructure data and media sentiment within a common HAR-based forecasting framework. Before the PhD, I spent seven years in industry applying the same underlying process now central to my research: taking an idea from hypothesis through to a tested, deployed result. This spanned equity factor research and multivariate time-series modeling for private-fund strategies, mean-variance portfolio construction for a wealth-management platform, and asset-backed securities pricing and valuation.

See [Publications](/publications/) for current working papers, [Talks](/talks/) for conference presentations, and [CV](/cv/) for full details.
